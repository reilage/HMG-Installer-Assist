# HMG R290 Exclusion Zone Guidance

Cite as **"HMG R290 exclusion zone guidance"**, plus the manufacturer's page. HMG uses each manufacturer's own figures as they are (Donal Reilly, 26/10/2026). Shown in the app under Install › Exclusion zones, for the R290 units only: the Panasonic L-series and the Baxi Assure HP55.

R290 (propane) is extremely flammable (safety group A3) and heavier than air: a leak collects at ground level and in low points. The manufacturer defines a zone around the outdoor unit that has to stay clear. It is separate from the clearances, and both apply. Keep the units separate: never use one manufacturer's zone figures for the other.

---

## Panasonic L-series R290 outdoor unit (WH-WDG05LE5 / WDG07LE5 / WDG09LE5): "protective zone" (L-series SM p.168)

What the manual says (12.7.2 Protective Zone, p.168):
- No building openings in the zone: windows, doors, light shafts, cellar entrances, escape hatches, flat-roof windows or ventilation openings.
- No ignition sources in the zone: heat above 360 °C, sparks, open flame, plug sockets, light switches, lamps, electrical switches or other permanent ignition sources.
- The zone must not extend onto neighbouring property or public areas: neighbours' boundaries, the public road, neighbours' private roads, subsidence areas, depressions, pump shafts, sewer intakes, waste water shafts and so on.
- Prevent refrigerant gathering anywhere dangerous, getting into the building through openings, or collecting in drain grooves.
- No later building work that breaks these rules inside the zone.
- On a wall-mounted unit the zone under the unit extends down to the floor.

Figures (letters as marked on the manual drawings, p.168):

| Installation | Figures |
|---|---|
| 1. Ground or flat roof, open area | A 1000 mm all round the unit |
| 2. Ground, in front of a building wall | A 2000 mm, B 3000 mm, C 300 mm, D 1000 mm |
| 3. Ground, in a building corner | A 2000 mm, B 2500 mm, C 300 mm, D 500 mm, E 1000 mm, F 500 mm, G 1800 mm |
| 4. Wall-mounted, in front of a building wall | A 2000 mm, B 3000 mm, C 300 mm, D 1000 mm (zone extends to the floor) |
| 5. Wall-mounted, in a building corner | A 2000 mm, B 2500 mm, C 300 mm, D 500 mm, E 1000 mm, F 500 mm, G 1800 mm (zone extends to the floor) |

Reading the drawings: in the elevation, A is the width of the zone at the top of the unit and B its width at ground level, so it widens towards the ground. In the plan view, C is behind the unit (to the wall) and on drawings 2 and 4 D is in front of it. On the corner drawings (3 and 5) use the letters as marked on the plan; G (1800 mm) is how far the zone reaches out in front. Always check against the drawing in the app.

Other R290 points from the L-series manual:
- Don't fit automatic air purge valves on the indoor water pipes: if R290 ever leaked into the water circuit it could get into the house (p.86).
- The connecting cable gland and terminal cover on the outdoor unit must be tightened to the stated torque, to stop gas getting in (p.171).
- Don't clean the outdoor unit with hydrocarbon-based solvents (p.172).
- Refrigerant work only by competent, certified people with R290 tools and procedures (pp.173-180).

## Baxi Assure HP55 R290 outdoor unit: "protective perimeter" (Baxi HP55 IM pp.30-31)

- Free-standing on the ground or a flat roof (Fig.18): 1000 mm all round the unit.
- In front of a wall (Fig.19): 300 mm behind (to the wall), 1000 mm in front. At the sides it is 500 mm at the top of the unit, widening to 1000 mm at ground level. Its height H is at least the height of the unit above the ground.
- In a corner (Fig.20): 300 mm behind, 500 mm to the side wall, 1000 mm on the open side at ground level, and it reaches up to 1800 mm in front.
- Inside the perimeter: no openings into the building (doors, windows, skylights, air vents, lightwells, cellar access, sewer hatches), no permanent ignition sources (lights, switches, sockets), no temporary ignition sources (lawnmowers, barbecues, cigarettes), and it must not adjoin alleys, parking spaces, neighbouring plots or public areas (p.30). It applies to wall-mounted units too and extends to the ground. Open condensate gullies inside it are not a safety risk (p.36).

## For every R290 install

- Mark the zone out on site before fixing the unit, and check nothing in the list above is inside it, including the neighbour's side of a boundary.
- Tell the customer the zone must stay clear for the life of the unit: no new windows, vents, sockets, outside lights or barbecues there later.
- If the zone can't be kept clear, don't install there: move the unit or escalate to Heat Merchants Technical Support.
