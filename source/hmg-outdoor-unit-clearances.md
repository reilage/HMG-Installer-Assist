# HMG Outdoor Unit Clearances

Cite as **"HMG clearance guidance"** (Donal Reilly, 24/10/2026), plus the manual page where the manual is quoted.

Minimum clear space from the outdoor unit to a wall, fence or other obstacle. Left and right are taken **looking at the front of the unit (the fan / air outlet side)**. Keep each unit separate: never quote one unit's figures for another.

## Cross-check against the manuals

### Panasonic J-series monobloc: the service manual gives no clearance figures

Checked against the original J-series Service Manual PDF on 2026-10-24. p.52 (12.1.1 Select the Best Location) says "keep minimum distance of spaces as illustrated above from wall, ceiling, or other obstacles", but the only drawing on the page is a sketch of the unit with three leader lines and **no dimensions or labels**. No other page in the 246-page manual gives clearance or service-space figures (searched the full text; p.38 is unit dimensions only: 1284 W × 865 H × 400 D mm). So the HMG figures (rear 300, right 300, left 100, front 1000) **cannot be confirmed or contradicted from this manual**: no differences to flag, but no backing either. The figures would be in the J-series *installation* manual, which is not on file.

Page image: `clearances/originals/panasonic-j-series-sm-p52-location-drawing.png`.

Other p.52 siting points from the manual:
- Outdoors only, on a flat solid surface; avoid sites below -20°C ambient.
- Good air circulation, easy drainage, accessible for maintenance, away from heat sources and flammable gas leaks.
- Don't obstruct the discharge air (no short-circuiting) and avoid the suction side facing straight into the wind.
- An awning must not block heat radiation from the condenser.
- Exposed or windy sites (rooftops, between buildings): fix with an overturn prevention wire (fitting K-KYZP15C).
- Fix to a concrete floor with M12 anchor bolts at 4 points, pull-out strength above 15000 N. Concrete compressive strength above 18 MPa.
- With the drain elbow: stand taller than 50 mm, cover the other 8 holes (ø20 mm) with rubber caps. Don't use the elbow and caps where it stays below 0°C for 2-3 days (drain water freezes and the fan won't turn).

### Baxi Assure HP55: manual asks for more on the sides, and more at the front on 11 and 13 kW

Baxi HP55 IM p.32 (6.2.2 Minimum distances from the walls, Fig.21 and 22):

| Baxi letter | Where | HP55-4/6/8 | HP55-11/13 | HMG figure | Match? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | Rear (back to wall) | ≥ 300 | ≥ 300 | 300 | Yes |
| B | Front (air outlet) | ≥ 1000 | **≥ 1500** | 1000 | Yes on 4/6/8. **No on 11/13: manual says 1500.** |
| G | Right-hand side | ≥ 500 | ≥ 500 | 300 | **No: manual says 500** |
| F / H | Left-hand side | ≥ 500 | ≥ 500 | 100 | **No: manual says 500** |
| A | Base above ground | ≥ 100 | ≥ 100 | none given | Manual only. Allow for snow (footnote, p.32). |
| C | Top of unit to an obstacle above | ≥ 500 | ≥ 500 | none given | Manual only |
| D | Overhang of an obstacle above, past the unit | ≤ 500 | ≤ 500 | none given | Manual only |

Between units (p.33, Fig.23): back to back ≥ 600 mm (V); front to front ≥ 2500 mm on 4/6/8, ≥ 3000 mm on 11/13 (W); side by side ≥ 500 mm (Y).
Cold climates (p.34): back of the unit towards the wall; at least 100 mm between the unit and the mean snow cover.

**HMG decision (Donal Reilly, 24/10/2026): installers follow the HMG figures.** Give the Baxi manual figures as a note only.

**R290 protective perimeter (Baxi HP55 IM pp.30-31).** Separate from the clearances above, and applies as well:
- Free-standing on the ground or a flat roof (Fig.18): 1000 mm all round the unit.
- In front of a wall (Fig.19): 300 mm behind (to the wall), 1000 mm in front. At the sides it is 500 mm at the top of the unit, widening to 1000 mm at ground level. Its height H is at least the height of the unit above the ground.
- In a corner (Fig.20): 300 mm behind, 500 mm to the side wall, 1000 mm on the open side at ground level, and it extends up to 1800 mm in front. See the drawing on p.31.
- Inside the perimeter: no openings into the building (doors, windows, skylights, air vents, lightwells, cellar access, sewer hatches), no permanent ignition sources (lights, switches, sockets), no temporary ignition sources (lawnmowers, barbecues, cigarettes), and it must not adjoin alleys, parking spaces, neighbouring plots or public areas (p.30). It applies to wall-mounted units too and extends to the ground. Open condensate gullies inside it are not a safety risk (p.36).
- R290 is highly flammable (A3) and heavier than air, so it collects at ground level.
