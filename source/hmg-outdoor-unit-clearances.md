# HMG Outdoor Unit Clearances

Cite as **"HMG clearance guidance"** (Donal Reilly, 24/10/2026), plus the manual page where the manual is quoted.

Minimum clear space from the outdoor unit to a wall, fence or other obstacle. Left and right are taken **looking at the front of the unit (the fan / air outlet side)**. Keep each unit separate: never quote one unit's figures for another.

## Cross-check against the manuals

### Panasonic J-series monobloc: the service manual gives no clearance figures

Checked against the original J-series Service Manual PDF on 2026-10-24. p.52 (12.1.1 Select the Best Location) says "keep minimum distance of spaces as illustrated above from wall, ceiling, or other obstacles", but the only drawing on the page is a sketch of the unit with three leader lines and **no dimensions or labels**. No other page in the 246-page manual gives clearance or service-space figures (searched the full text; p.38 is unit dimensions only: 1284 W × 865 H × 400 D mm). So the HMG figures (rear 300, right 300, left 100, front 1000) **cannot be confirmed or contradicted from this manual**: no differences to flag, but no backing either. The figures would be in the J-series *installation* manual, which is not on file.

Page image: `clearances/originals/panasonic-j-series-sm-p52-location-drawing.png`.

Other p.52 siting points from the manual:
- Outdoors only, on a flat solid surface; avoid sites below -20°C ambient.
- Good air circulation, easy drainage, accessible for maintenance, away from heat sources and flammable gas leaks.
- Don't obstruct the discharge air (no short-circuiting) and avoid the suction side facing straight into the wind.
- An awning must not block heat radiation from the condenser.
- Exposed or windy sites (rooftops, between buildings): fix with an overturn prevention wire (fitting K-KYZP15C).
- Fix to a concrete floor with M12 anchor bolts at 4 points, pull-out strength above 15000 N. Concrete compressive strength above 18 MPa.
- With the drain elbow: stand taller than 50 mm, cover the other 8 holes (ø20 mm) with rubber caps. Don't use the elbow and caps where it stays below 0°C for 2-3 days (drain water freezes and the fan won't turn).

### Baxi Assure HP55: manual asks for more on the sides, and more at the front on 11 and 13 kW

Baxi HP55 IM p.32 (6.2.2 Minimum distances from the walls, Fig.21 and 22):

| Baxi letter | Where | HP55-4/6/8 | HP55-11/13 | HMG figure | Match? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | Rear (back to wall) | ≥ 300 | ≥ 300 | 300 | Yes |
| B | Front (air outlet) | ≥ 1000 | **≥ 1500** | 1000 | Yes on 4/6/8. **No on 11/13: manual says 1500.** |
| G | Right-hand side | ≥ 500 | ≥ 500 | 300 | **No: manual says 500** |
| F / H | Left-hand side | ≥ 500 | ≥ 500 | 100 | **No: manual says 500** |
| A | Base above ground | ≥ 100 | ≥ 100 | none given | Manual only. Allow for snow (footnote, p.32). |
| C | Top of unit to an obstacle above | ≥ 500 | ≥ 500 | none given | Manual only |
| D | Overhang of an obstacle above, past the unit | ≤ 500 | ≤ 500 | none given | Manual only |

Between units (p.33, Fig.23): back to back ≥ 600 mm (V); front to front ≥ 2500 mm on 4/6/8, ≥ 3000 mm on 11/13 (W); side by side ≥ 500 mm (Y).
Cold climates (p.34): back of the unit towards the wall; at least 100 mm between the unit and the mean snow cover.

**HMG decision (Donal Reilly, 24/10/2026): installers follow the HMG figures.** Give the Baxi manual figures as a note only.

**R290 protective perimeter (Baxi HP55 IM pp.30-31).** Separate from the clearances above, and applies as well:
- Free-standing on the ground or a flat roof (Fig.18): 1000 mm all round the unit.
- In front of a wall (Fig.19): 300 mm behind (to the wall), 1000 mm in front. At the sides it is 500 mm at the top of the unit, widening to 1000 mm at ground level. Its height H is at least the height of the unit above the ground.
- In a corner (Fig.20): 300 mm behind, 500 mm to the side wall, 1000 mm on the open side at ground level, and it extends up to 1800 mm in front. See the drawing on p.31.
- Inside the perimeter: no openings into the building (doors, windows, skylights, air vents, lightwells, cellar access, sewer hatches), no permanent ignition sources (lights, switches, sockets), no temporary ignition sources (lawnmowers, barbecues, cigarettes), and it must not adjoin alleys, parking spaces, neighbouring plots or public areas (p.30). It applies to wall-mounted units too and extends to the ground. Open condensate gullies inside it are not a safety risk (p.36).
- R290 is highly flammable (A3) and heavier than air, so it collects at ground level.

### Panasonic K-series outdoor unit: the service manual agrees with HMG

Checked against the K-series Service Manual (Order No PCZ2508021CE, 2025) on 2026-10-26. p.94 (12.3.2.1 Installation Diagram) shows the same clearances for both drawings (WH-UDZ03KE5, and WH-UDZ05/07/09KE5): **100 mm rear, 100 mm left-hand side, 300 mm right-hand side, 1000 mm front**, all "or more". That is exactly the HMG figure, so there are no differences to flag.

Page image: `clearances/originals/panasonic-k-series-sm-p94-installation-diagram.png`.

Other siting points from the manual (cite "K-series SM"):
- Avoid more than 2 blocked directions; for multiple outdoor units, consult the dealer (p.94).
- Fix the unit firmly and level on concrete or a rigid frame with ø10 mm bolts and nuts. On a roof, allow for strong wind (p.94).
- With the drain elbow: a stand taller than 50 mm and rubber caps on the other ø20 mm holes (7 on the 3 kW, 3 on the 5-9 kW). Where it stays below 0 °C for 2 or 3 days running, don't use the drain elbow and caps: the drain water freezes and the fan won't turn (p.94).
- Avoid sites that drop below -20 °C (3 kW) or -25 °C (5, 7 and 9 kW). Don't block the discharge air or let it short-circuit. An awning must not block the heat radiation from the coil (p.93).
- A base pan heater (CZ-NE2P, optional) is strongly recommended in cold areas (p.93).
- 5, 7 and 9 kW: fit the discharge grille (supplied) before powering on (p.95).

### Panasonic J-series T-Cap monobloc: the service manual gives the same as the HMG J-series figures

Checked against the T-Cap Service Manual (Order No PAPAMY2101001CE, 2021) on 2026-10-26. p.49 (12.1 Mono Bloc Unit, dimension diagram) shows the clearances on a 3D view of the unit, all "or more": **300 mm rear, 300 mm right-hand side, 100 mm left-hand side (the fan end), 1000 mm front**. That is the same as HMG's J-series Mini Monobloc figures.

Other siting points from the manual (cite "T-Cap SM"):
- Avoid more than 2 blocked sides (p.34).
- Outdoors only, on a flat solid surface; avoid sites below -20°C ambient (p.50).
- Good air circulation, easy drainage, accessible for maintenance, away from heat sources and flammable gas leaks; don't block the discharge air or face the suction side into the wind (p.50).
- Exposed or windy sites: fix with an overturn prevention wire (fitting K-KYZP15C) (p.50).
- Fix to a concrete floor with M12 anchor bolts at 4 points, pull-out strength above 15000 N. Concrete compressive strength above 18 MPa (p.50). The unit weighs 140 kg empty (p.11), more when full of water.
- With the drain elbow: stand taller than 50 mm, cover the 8 holes (ø20 mm) with rubber caps. Don't use the elbow and caps where it stays below 0°C for 2-3 days (drain water freezes and the fan won't turn) (p.50).
- A base pan heater (CZ-NE3P) is strongly recommended in cold climates (p.49, p.60).

### Panasonic L-series R290 outdoor unit: the service manual agrees with HMG

Checked against the L-series Service Manual (Order No PCZ2504005CE, 2025) on 2026-10-26. p.169 (12.7.3.1 Dimension Diagram) shows **300 mm rear, 300 mm right-hand side, 100 mm left-hand side, 1000 mm front**, all "or more", the same as the HMG figures. HMG confirmed them (Donal Reilly 26/10/2026).

Other siting points from the manual (cite "L-series SM"):
- Avoid more than 2 blocked directions; for multiple outdoor units, consult the dealer (p.169).
- Avoid sites that drop below -25 °C. Don't block the discharge air or let it short-circuit. An awning must not block the heat radiation from the coil (p.167).
- Fix the unit upright and level on concrete or a solid surface with M10 or W 3/8 bolts and nuts; on a roof, allow for strong wind (p.169).
- With the drain elbow: a stand taller than 50 mm and rubber caps on the ø32 mm holes. Where it stays below 0 °C for 2 or 3 days running, don't use the drain elbow and caps: the drain water freezes and the fan won't turn (p.170).
- Pressure relief valve drain: 15 mm hose, running continuously downward to open, frost-free air, outlet where it can't be blocked, not into a sewer or drain that may give off gas (p.170).
- A base pan heater (CZ-NE4P, optional) is strongly recommended in cold areas (p.167).
- Fit the discharge grilles before powering on (p.170, p.172).
- Water pipes between the outdoor unit and tank unit: ø20 (5 kW) or ø25 (7/9 kW) inner diameter, 30 m maximum, 10 m maximum height difference, 30 mm or more insulation (p.167).
- R290: a protective zone applies around the outdoor unit (p.168). See the HMG R290 exclusion zone guidance.
