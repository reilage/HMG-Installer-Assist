# HMG Quick Survey (heat pump size and radiators from the BER)

Cite as **"HMG quick survey guidance"** (Donal Reilly, 27/10/2026). A quick estimate so an installer can request an HMG quote fast. **It is not a design.**

> **Disclaimer (show on every result):** Quick estimate only, from the BER Heat Loss Indicator. A full heat loss design is required for designer sign-off and for HMG professional indemnity insurance before the system is installed.

---

## 1. Heat pump size

- The installer gives the **total floor area** (m²) and the **HLI** (Heat Loss Indicator, W/K per m²) from the BER assessment (DEAP).
- Design heat loss (W) = HLI × floor area × design ΔT.
- Floor area: the installer either types the BER total floor area, or adds the rooms and the app totals them (Donal Reilly, 27/10/2026). When totalling rooms, include halls, landings and every other room.
- Design ΔT = inside 21 °C − outside −3 °C = **24 K**, unless the installer gives other temperatures.
- Example: HLI 2.0 × 200 m² × 24 K = 9,600 W = 9.6 kW.

### No BER: HLI from the year built (Donal Reilly, 28/10/2026)

If the installer has no BER HLI, they can pick the year the house was built and the survey uses a typical HLI for that age (the middle of the range, for a home as built). **A BER HLI, when given, always overrides the year built.** Say the HLI is an estimate and that wall, attic or window upgrades lower it, so the BER or a heat loss calculation should confirm it.

| Year built | Regulations at the time | Typical HLI range | HLI used | About W/m² at 24 K |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before 1919 | None: solid stone or brick walls | 3.5–5.0 | 4.2 | 100 |
| 1919–1978 | No thermal regulations: solid or unfilled cavity walls | 3.0–4.5 | 3.7 | 89 |
| 1979–1991 | First thermal regulations (walls U about 1.1) | 2.5–3.5 | 3.0 | 72 |
| 1992–2004 | TGD L 1991/1997 (walls U 0.55–0.45) | 2.0–2.8 | 2.4 | 58 |
| 2005–2010 | TGD L 2005/2008 (walls U 0.27) | 1.4–2.0 | 1.7 | 41 |
| 2011–2018 | TGD L 2011 (around A3) | 1.0–1.5 | 1.3 | 31 |
| 2019 on | TGD L 2019 NZEB (A2) | 0.7–1.2 | 1.0 | 24 |

## 3. Rooms and radiators

- The installer lists each room with its length and width (m) and type.
- Room heat loss (W) = HLI × room floor area × (room temperature − outside temperature).
- Room temperature: **21 °C for every room** by default (HMG, Donal Reilly 27/10/2026). The installer can change any room.
- Rooms not listed (e.g. a landing with no radiator) still count in the heat pump size, which uses the whole floor area at 21 °C.
- Radiator per room from the HMG radiator list (Warmhaus) at 45 °C flow, 5 °C drop, at that room's temperature: the closest Type 22 above the room heat loss, at the radiator height and within any maximum length. **One radiator height for the whole house** (600 mm unless the installer picks another), not a mix of heights; a room can have its own height when it needs one, e.g. under a window (HMG, Donal Reilly 27/10/2026). If no single Type 22 fits, two Type 22s that add up to it, otherwise a Type 33. When two Type 22s would be needed and a single Type 33 in the same space is enough, the installer can choose the one Type 33 instead, so an existing house doesn't need a second radiator in the room (HMG, Donal Reilly 30/10/2026).

## 4. Hot water cylinder

HMG cylinders (Donal Reilly, 27/10/2026), sized by bedrooms:

- **Up to 3 bedrooms: 200 L.** 4 to 7 bedrooms: **300 L.** Over 7 bedrooms: talk to HMG.
- **Pre-plumbed (Xylinder):** 200 L UB7317, 300 L UB7318.
- **Not pre-plumbed (Panasonic Aquarea):** 200 L U95447, 300 L U95448. Always supplied with its kit: 2 × motorised valve U37935, bypass valve U07346, unvented kit U94515, 3-way valve UB7176.
- **Only the J-series, T-Cap and Baxi get a cylinder.** The **L-series and K-series** hold their hot water in the 180 L all-in-one indoor unit, so no separate cylinder. If the bedrooms call for 300 L, talk to HMG.

## 5. Request an HMG quote

Design › Quick survey has **Request HMG Design**, then **Send Email & Quick Design**: it sends HMG Technical Design (donal.reilly@heatmerchants.ie) a written request for a full SR 50 design, with the quotation PDF (survey, quote list, prices and product data sheets) and the quote list CSV attached, plus any floor plans or sketches the installer adds (photos or PDFs, photos shrunk on the phone), and the installer's details. **Download PDF** gives the installer the quotation on its own.
